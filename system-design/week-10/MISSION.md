# Mission: Explain and improve the whole AI backend

## Why
Independently design, debug and explain a Python AI backend whose dependencies can fail, whose performance is measured, and whose data boundaries are explicit. Connect the earlier API, storage, jobs, RAG and agent pieces with defensible decisions before deployment.

## Success looks like
- Trace request/data flow across five consistent diagram views.
- Implement and test safe request-correlated AI events.
- Defend retry, queue, cache, rate-limit and component choices.
- Measure a bottleneck, reproduce a failure and explain a practical mitigation.
- Review ordinary backend and AI-specific threats with testable controls.

## Constraints
- Exposure to Python/basic Git, without demonstrated independent mastery.
- Earlier RAG/agent starters are unfinished; fixture and real integration must be distinguished.
- Flexible pacing; small worked examples before independent tasks.
- Local, CPU-only, no paid provisioning, publishing, account changes or external messages.

## Out of scope
- DSA, NeetCode, interview algorithms, complexity drills and their logs.
- Detailed future-week deployment, training models or full earlier-week reconstruction.
