# Use AI to strengthen ownership
Before asking: predict, attempt, run the smallest check, then show the actual error and your explanation.
Ask for one hint, a counterexample, a source explanation or review. Do not ask for completed instrumentation.py or four finished ADRs before your own attempt.
Record assistance beside each evidence entry. Rebuild or vary the idea later without hints.
Do not paste credentials, private prompts, document contents or personal identifiers into questions.
Only learner evidence can mark progress. Author validation cannot.
