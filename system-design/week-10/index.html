The learning path

  1. 01Establish what actually runsTrace one request and separate existing evidence from assumptions.
  2. 02Give a module one reason to changeInject a small interface and explain who owns policy versus storage.
  3. 03Draw the system at two useful zoom levelsCreate context and container views without claiming unbuilt components exist.
  4. 04Trace time, data and deployment separatelyFinish sequence, data-flow and deployment views that answer different questions.
  5. 05Bound a remote failureChoose retries using deadlines, repeatability and observable failure.
  6. 06Survive duplicate and dead jobsDescribe a retry-safe job transition and a controlled replay.
  7. 07Read logs, metrics and traces togetherUse a request ID to turn a symptom into a testable hypothesis.
  8. 08Instrument a seam without leaking contentImplement request-correlated AI logs and test both outcomes.
  9. 09Design cache and rate-limit behaviorSpecify tenant-safe keys, staleness and atomic quotas.
  10. 10Measure before choosing an optimizationRun a controlled latency experiment and separate waiting from work.
  11. 11Review every trust boundaryTurn backend and AI threats into controls with falsifiable checks.
  12. 12Write decisions an operator can useProduce four reasoned ADRs, an actionable runbook and a reviewable change description.
  13. 13Prove ownership after the hints fadeComplete an independent implementation, debugging and explain-back review.
