# AI as your teacher

1. Try for 10–15 focused minutes. Write your prediction and actual behavior.
2. Ask for one hint, a smaller explanation, or a review of your own attempt.
3. For an error, share the command, working directory, relevant code, and traceback with secrets removed.
4. Read the cited primary documentation and change one input yourself.
5. Explain every line you keep. Rebuild the key idea later without the previous answer.

Useful requests:
- “Here is my attempt. Ask me one question that helps me find the bug.”
- “Explain this traceback, but let me make the fix.”
- “Review my function against the contract and suggest one missing case.”
- “Quiz me on paths without showing the answers first.”

A working AI-written solution is not a completed learning exercise. Supplied wrappers/tests are teaching infrastructure: trace them, then gradually take ownership of them too.
