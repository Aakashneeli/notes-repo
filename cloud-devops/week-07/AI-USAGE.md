# Use AI as a tutor
Before requesting help, write your prediction, exact starting directory, command and observed error. Ask for one hint or a smaller example first. Try the correction yourself and explain why it worked.
Do not request a completed Dockerfile, Compose file, middleware or assessed CI workflow. AI may review your attempt and point to a failing contract. Record help used in weekly-logs/week-07.md; explain every copied line before claiming independence.
Never paste credentials, private uploads or raw environment dumps into a prompt or community post. All supplied credentials and sample text are disposable public lab data.
