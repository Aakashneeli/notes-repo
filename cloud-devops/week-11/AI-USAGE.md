# AI assistance boundary
Predict first; ask for a hint or a trace before an implementation. Keep Dockerfile, CI decisions,
release gate and operational records learner-owned. Explain every accepted line.
Record prompts/help in weekly-logs/week-11.md. Never share keys, tokens, .env values or private data.
Request review of a redacted runbook rather than asking the tutor to provision services.
Author tests are preparation evidence, never learner mastery.
