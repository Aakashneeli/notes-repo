Find a file when you need it

This map is introduced in lesson 1. Read each item in its first relevant lesson for purpose and actions. It covers supplied files, not every installed dependency. Folder groups: lessons/reference/assets = teaching and shared presentation; plan = sequence/coverage/evidence criteria; practice = examples and drills; projects = assessed API; templates = prompts to reuse; weekly-logs/codebase-reading = learner evidence; roadmap = brief source context; maintenance = tutor checks.

Generated .venv, caches and package metadata are explained in lesson 2 and ignored in Git. Learner-created outputs are introduced in lessons 12–14: final Postman export, request-lifecycle.md and request-sequence.mmd. They do not exist until you do the work; no fake evidence files are supplied.
