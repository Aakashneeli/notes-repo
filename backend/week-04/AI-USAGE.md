# Assistance that builds ownership

Before asking: state the intended behavior, prediction, exact command, actual result,
and smallest relevant code. Ask for one hint or a traceback explanation first.
Completed greeting/bridge examples are teaching aids; Task API implementations and
decisions are yours. Do not paste a generated complete service into the starter.
Log substantial hints, copied fragments and what you reconstructed independently.
A tutor may review tests, suggest counterexamples and explain mechanisms without
claiming you mastered them. No tool output automatically checks progress.
