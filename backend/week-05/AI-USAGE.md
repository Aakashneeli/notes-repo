# Assistance agreement
Predict before running; attempt before asking for code. Ask for one hint, an explanation of
a traceback or a review of your query. Keep main model/repository/migration decisions yours.
In weekly-logs/week-05.md record assistance and which lines you can explain.
After help, close the answer, reconstruct the small mechanism and vary an input.
Never paste database credentials or personal data into a community question.
Only the learner may choose to post a sanitized question; this workspace sends nothing.
